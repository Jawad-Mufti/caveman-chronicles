# Ugu the Unbowed — era progression (design reference)

Jawad's design (2026-10-09). The levels, their eras, mechanics, weapons, bosses, gems and costumes.
The shelter follows it: `docs/shelter_plan.md`.

Premise: one unchanged caveman (Ugu) is flung forward through human history by a time rift
that opens at the end of Level 3. Every era tries to civilize him and fails ("Unbowed").
Civilization rises, then collapses back to the wild, where Ugu is king. Full circle.

Design rules:
- The WORLD of each era is historically accurate (buildings, weapons, clothing, animals).
- BOSSES may be real animals, extinct giants, or creatures of myth/legend (type noted below).
- Every weapon is unique: its own swing style + its own special (hold attack, release).
- Every level hides one gem; the era's blacksmith (a descendant of the one-armed Toolmaker)
  forges it into a secret weapon that counters that level's boss.
- Every level has its own signature way of moving, so no two levels play the same.
- v1 launch = Levels 1–3 + the Home hub; the time rift at the end of Level 3 is the cliffhanger.
