# Ugu the Unbowed — weapons by era (design reference)

Jawad's design (2026-10-09); more may be added later. It is the source of truth for weapons: where it differs
from the weapon columns of `docs/eras.md`, this one is newer (see the notes at the end).

Rules:
- Every era gives Ugu TWO weapons from its own time:
  1. a CASUAL everyday weapon (earned/bought in that era), and
  2. a SPECIAL weapon, forged by the era's blacksmith from the level's hidden gem;
     it is designed to counter that level's boss.
- Both weapons are historically true to the era (future eras: plausible tech).
- Every weapon is unique: its own swing style + its own move when the player holds
  attack and releases (same system as the club's Home Run / axe's Axe Throw).
- Once earned, every weapon carries forward; the player picks what to carry.
- Levels 1–2 are built: club, Flint Axe and Firestone Hammer already exist.

  every weapon would make the hold on J the rule for all of them.
